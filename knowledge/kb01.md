# PA Hazardous Waste Generator Rules (Summary for CleanSite)

**Category:** Regulatory / compliance
**Primary source:** PA DEP, "Hazardous Waste Frequently Asked Questions" (pa.gov, DEP Hazardous Waste Program). Retrieved September 18, 2026.
**Also drawn from:** search-result excerpts of DEP's generator pages and Very Small / Small Quantity Generator fact sheets (verify against the full documents).
**Note:** This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. DEP itself says its FAQ answers are generalized and don't apply in every circumstance.

## 1. Household vs. business waste
- DEP states that hazardous waste from a household is exempt from hazardous waste regulation in Pennsylvania.
- Businesses that generate hazardous waste are regulated. Which category they fall into depends on how much they generate per month.
- GAP: The DEP FAQ does not say whether leftover paint from a contractor working at a customer's home counts as household waste. CleanSite should not answer this. Refer the user to DEP.

## 2. Generator categories (by hazardous waste generated per calendar month)
- Very Small Quantity Generator (VSQG): 220 lb (100 kg) or less
- Small Quantity Generator (SQG): more than 220 lb, less than 2,200 lb
- Large Quantity Generator (LQG): 2,200 lb or more
- The generator must first determine whether its waste is actually hazardous (listed, or has a hazardous characteristic such as ignitability).

## 3. Transporting waste
- A VSQG may haul its own hazardous waste in PA without a hazardous waste transporter license. It must still mark containers "Hazardous Waste" and label them to identify the hazards (DOT rules).
- SQGs and LQGs must hold their own PA transporter license or hire a licensed PA hazardous waste transporter.

## 4. Manifests and EPA ID numbers
- VSQGs are not required by regulation to use a manifest, but most transporters and disposal facilities require one before accepting waste.
- SQGs need an EPA ID number (EPA Form 8700-12, sent to DEP) and must use a manifest for off-site shipments.
- Waste from an episodic event that a VSQG ships off-site must follow manifest rules.

## 5. Universal wastes relevant to painters
- PA recognizes **oil-based finishes** as universal wastes (25 Pa. Code Chapter 266b). This is a reduced-requirement pathway meant to encourage proper collection and recycling or disposal.
- **Aerosol cans** (including spray paint) that are hazardous waste became universal wastes on February 7, 2020. Handlers must keep them in sound containers and prevent releases. Puncturing cans requires a written procedure, and the contents are then handled as hazardous waste.
- GAP: The detailed handler requirements for oil-based finishes are in 25 Pa. Code Chapter 266b, which is not yet in this knowledge base.

## 6. Solvent-contaminated rags and wipes
- PA follows the federal solvent-contaminated wipes rule. Qualifying wipes are conditionally excluded from hazardous waste rules.
- Conditions: non-leaking closed containers labeled "Excluded Solvent-Contaminated Wipes"; stored on-site no more than 180 days; no free liquids when sent out; reusable wipes go to a laundry regulated for its discharge; disposable wipes go to a regulated landfill or combustion facility.
- Wipes containing trichloroethylene are not covered by the exclusion.

## 7. Temporary spikes in waste
- Episodic events: a VSQG or SQG can exceed its monthly limit once per calendar year (unless DEP grants a petition) and keep its category. It needs an EPA ID and must notify DEP (30 days ahead if planned; within 72 hours if unplanned).
- Short-term generators (one-time, non-recurring events) can get a temporary EPA ID number, valid no longer than 180 days.

## 8. Accumulating waste at the work point
- Satellite accumulation: up to 55 gallons total (across all wastes) at or near the point of generation, under the operator's control.

## 9. When CleanSite should send users to DEP
- Any question on whether a specific job's waste counts as household or business waste
- Anything about generator status changes, EPA IDs, or manifests beyond the basics above
- DEP Hazardous Waste Division: 717-787-6239, RA-HazWaste@pa.gov
