# Centre County Paint and Hazardous Waste Disposal Options (Summary for CleanSite)

**Category:** Existing solution analysis + best practices
**Sources:**
1. CCRRA "Rules and Regulations" (effective April 28, 2008; modified 2024)
2. CCRRA "Paint" guidance page (undated)
3. CCRRA "What to Bring" flyer for the household hazardous waste (HHW) event (undated)
4. StateCollege.com news coverage of past HHW events (various years)

**Contact:** Centre County Recycling and Refuse Authority (CCRRA), 253 Transfer Road, Bellefonte, PA 16823. Phone 814-238-7005 (from the flyer).
**Note:** Plain-language summary, not legal advice.

## 1. Facilities and hours (from the Rules and Regulations)
- Transfer Station: Monday-Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
- Closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. A Saturday holiday is observed Friday; a Sunday holiday is observed Monday.
- CCRRA may change hours without notice. Users should call to confirm.

## 2. Paint guidance (from the CCRRA Paint page)
- Best option: use the paint up or give it away (theater groups, church groups, shelters, community organizations). Habitat for Humanity ReStore accepts latex paint; call (814) 353-2390 for specifications.
- Storage: keep lids on tight; label each can with color and purchase date.
- Never pour paint down household drains. Never put liquid paint in the trash (CCRRA warns of release from the can and spontaneous combustion risk).
- Small leftover amount: remove the lid outside in good ventilation and let it dry. The dried can can go in the trash. An empty can can be recycled with metal cans.
- Larger amounts: pour into a sturdy box of clay-based kitty litter, or use a store-bought paint solidifier. When dry, put the mixture out with the trash.
- **Latex paint:** dried latex can go in regular trash. The Transfer Station accepts up to 5 gallons of latex paint per person per day, with a $25.00 minimum charge. The HHW event does not accept latex paint.
- **Oil-based paint:** dried oil-based paint can go in regular trash. The Transfer Station does not accept liquid oil-based paint. CCRRA accepts it at its spring HHW collection event.

## 3. Annual HHW collection event
- Held once a year in spring at the CCRRA site. Households only.
- Per news coverage (not CCRRA directly): free to residents, pre-registration has been required, and the most recent article says the next event is spring 2027. Verify before relying on this.
- Trade-relevant items on the "What to Bring" flyer: oil-based paints, oil-based stains, oil-based varnishes, paint thinner, spray paint, roof coating, driveway sealer, furniture stripper, degreasers/parts cleaners, linseed oil, kerosene, and other flammables.
- Latex paint is not on the flyer, and news coverage says it is not accepted at the event.

## 4. CCRRA rules relevant to paint and solvents
- CCRRA defines household hazardous waste as material that would be hazardous under Pennsylvania's Solid Waste Management Act except that it comes in small quantities from persons not otherwise covered as hazardous waste generators. The list includes paints, stains, wood preservatives, thinners, solvents, and furniture strippers.
- Unacceptable at CCRRA facilities: hazardous waste; drums, barrels, buckets, and paint cans unless lids are removed and interiors are cleaned free of residue; liquid waste (less than 20% solids or flowable).
- Consequences: anyone delivering unacceptable waste pays the tipping fee plus removal, cleanup, and CCRRA's costs, and may face fines or suspension of facility privileges.
- Construction/demolition waste (including drywall) is regulated municipal waste with its own handling rules.

## 5. Gaps and conflicts (CleanSite must not guess these)
1. **Contractor eligibility:** The Paint page is written for households, and CCRRA's HHW definition excludes persons covered as hazardous waste generators. No source says whether a contractor's leftover paint qualifies for the HHW event or the Transfer Station latex service. Refer to CCRRA (814-238-7005) or PA DEP.
2. **Transfer Station conflict:** The Rules and Regulations list HHW among wastes that may be delivered to the Transfer Station, but the Paint page says liquid oil-based paint is not accepted there. Treat the Paint page as more specific and advise confirming by phone.
3. **Event date and registration:** Comes from news coverage, not CCRRA. Advise verifying.
4. **Document dates:** The Paint page and flyer are undated.
5. **Not covered:** Centre County guidance on solvent-soaked rags and on aerosol cans other than the flyer's mention of spray paint.
